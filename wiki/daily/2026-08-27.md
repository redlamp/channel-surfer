# 2026-08-27

## The resolution mystery, closed

Taylor's report — *"some of the tiles look much lower resolution than the
source"* — resolved to **JPEG 4:2:0 chroma subsampling in the source
files**, not anything the app does. The split Taylor observed (Source and
RGB channels clean; Warm/cool, Hue·flat·steps, Saturation, Chroma blocky)
is exactly the luma-cancellation line. Full story + measurements in
[[decision-chroma-subsampling-response]].

The new higher-res Lady Agnew (5000×6324) is *still* 4:2:0 — header
declares it; 57.7% of 2×2 blocks are constant colour over varying
brightness. Better in absolute colour detail, same at 1:1.

## Warm/cool grows a legend and a context bar (late morning)

Three rapid-fire asks from Taylor, all on the warm/cool tile:

- **Hexagon legend ring.** Hovering a warm/cool tile now puts the same
  legend ring the hue map gets around the Color Hex, painted with the
  warm/cool mapping (warm centred on 45°, cool on 225°, transition bands
  at 135°/315°) so the split lines up against the R/Y/G/C/B/M corners.
  Both ring builders were refactored onto one `ringGradientFor` helper,
  and both now respect the Gamma setting (previously the twilight ring
  always did the linear→sRGB convert, mismatching tiles under sRGB math).
- **Palette toggle** — built, verified, and removed within the hour:
  Taylor tried `Orange / Blue` vs `Twilight` (the hue-map ramp's arms)
  and called it — orange/blue wins, the toggle goes. The shader keeps
  the fixed accents; the context bar keeps only Flat/Shaded (relabelled
  "Shade").
- **Flat / Shaded toggle** (same bar, label "Shade"): `uWarmCoolShade`,
  cross-faded. The Shaded blend went through three designs in an hour,
  each a Taylor correction on the last:
  1. *Multiply, near-black neutrals* — neutrals crushed to black,
     erasing the shading the variant exists to show.
  2. *Multiply, white neutrals* — neutrals became brightness greyscale;
     but flat mode's near-black floor still punched jarring holes, and a
     dim-ghost interim (neutral = 35% brightness) still didn't sit level
     with the accents.
  3. **Final: Photoshop Color blend.** Flat neutrals are grey at the
     accents' average Rec.601 luma (computed in-shader from the
     constants, ~0.60), so the neutral field sits level with the orange
     and blue. Shaded runs `setLum` + `ClipColor`: ink keeps hue and
     saturation, pixel keeps luminosity; neutral ink degenerates to
     luminance greyscale. Verified — the whole painting reads through
     the warm/cool overlay, no black voids in either mode.

The Tint bar plumbing generalized: `barGroupOfTile()` decides which
context bar a tile gets ("rgb" | "chroma" | "warmcool" | none).

## Gamma leaves the floating bar

Taylor weighed a notched Linear↔sRGB slider (the shader's `uSrgbMath`
is already a continuous cross-fade, so scrubbing was nearly free) but
the parked-middle problem — intermediate positions are no real color
space, and readouts compute strictly in one or the other — argued
against it. Decision: **Gamma stays a plain toggle, Settings-only,
sRGB default; the floating bar carries just Model.** A spring-loaded
scrubber (drag to morph, snap back to a notch) was sketched as the
have-both option if the itch returns.

## Framing survives image switches

A framed tile now stays framed when the image changes: the new-image
refit effect re-frames the same grid position against the new aspect
(snap, no tween — easing over a wholesale image change reads as drift)
instead of resetting to the full grid. Pin and peek still clear — the
pinned color belongs to the old image. The effect had to move below the
`frameTile`/`unframe` callbacks it now calls (dep-array TDZ, the same
trap this file hit twice yesterday). Verified: framed Warm/cool on the
portrait Lady Agnew, switched to 16:9 Silk Song — frame held, camera
refit.

## Demo rainbows back to lossless (+ WebP subsampling detection)

The library details called the rainbow demos "lossy" while
source-store's comment said lossless — both were telling the truth
about different commits: 5bb346e made them lossless, 94be973 re-encoded
lossy q95 for -91% size. Measured the shipped files: VP8 (inherently
4:2:0), drift <=6/255 straddling the neutral tolerance. For an app that
now badges 4:2:0 sources as colour-degraded, its own demos carrying the
artifact was untenable - and the size rationale was weak, since the
rainbows idle-defer and never block startup. Restored lossless
(byte-identical encode, roundtrip drift 0) and taught riffDetails what
the codec implies: lossy WebP = always 4:2:0, VP8L = 4:4:4, VP8X =
unknown. Verified in-app: "RGB(A), lossless · 4:4:4 (full res)".
