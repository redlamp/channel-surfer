# 2026-09-14

## Hue Tile Controls

- Combined shaded, flat, lit and mid into one selectable Hue tile with per-position saturation, flat/shaded and brightness controls.
- Migrated saved settings while preserving the previous rendering and Factorial preset.
- Added migration and shader pixel-readback coverage, including tile independence and persistence.
- Created [[decision-hue-tile-controls]].

## Hover Handoff

- Added an invisible bridge over the gap above tile controls so the next canvas tile cannot steal hover while moving into the panel. Applies to Hue and existing context bars.
- Browser regression reproduced the lost Hue controls before the fix and passes after it, including a pause longer than the hover grace period and dismissal when moving away.

## Hue Sliders

- Replaced saturation presets with a continuous slider, retaining Original saturation and Flat brightness options. Percent readouts use a fixed four-character width with space-padded monospaced digits.
- Added migration from preset names and checked arbitrary saturation values, zero saturation, readout stability and the hover bridge in browser tests.

- Renamed both Hue toggles to Uniform and inverted the saturation checkbox so checked consistently means a uniform value.

- Removed the unified Hue shader’s legacy neutral threshold at 100% saturation. Neutrals now follow brightness independently of saturation. Pixel tests compare neutral output at 99% and 100%.

- Hue control footer now describes both saturation and brightness states instead of brightness alone.

- Removed the Hue control footer at Taylor’s request.

## Library Left Panel

- Created [[decision-library-left-panel]]. Moved the library to its own left panel and header toggle; kept Inspect and Settings on the right.
- Updated canvas fitting for both side panels and narrow-screen panel switching.
- Verified desktop/mobile layouts and independent toggles in browser tests; all eight browser/shader tests pass.

- Moved library/inspector toggles out of the header into floating buttons just below it at the left/right edges; lowered desktop panels to clear the buttons.

- Attached panel toggles to their inner edges and restored panel height. Removed automatic refitting on panel changes; outside double-click still fits around open panels. New-image fits ignore side panels by default. Verified stationary camera across toggles and explicit refitting, with all eight browser tests passing.

- Matched expand/collapse button colors, removed inner button rounding at panel seams, and added a 180ms slide for panels and toggles. Closed panels are inert and hidden after the exit animation; reduced-motion preferences disable transitions.

- Changed side-menu and toggle easing to power2.inOut, sampled as a shared CSS linear() curve; kept the 180ms duration and reduced-motion behavior.
