## Hue tile exploration (evening, uncommitted branch)

Taylor: *"let's explore alternative ideas for the N, NW, W tiles (using
compass positioning), what are some alternative ways to show Hue?"*

The prompt for it: on any real image **N (`Hue · shaded`) and NE
(`Hue · flat`) look nearly identical** — both max saturation, differing only
in whether brightness survives.

- **Taylor's factorial.** N = max sat/keep brightness, NE = keep sat/max
  brightness, W = max both. Source is already the fourth cell, so
  source / shaded / lit / flat is every combination of {saturation,
  brightness} × {kept, maxed}. Costs the hue map its tile — unresolved
  where it should go.
- **`lit` blew out** — maxing brightness sends every low-saturation pixel to
  white; roughly a third of a Silksong frame turned to flat white. Replaced
  by **`mid`** (brightness pinned to one level, default 70%, tunable). Same
  job, no clipping. At 100% it reproduces `lit` exactly, which is how the
  slider was verified.
- **HSB vs HSL side by side.** Taylor: *"How about N is S from HSL, and NE
  is L from HSL?"* Four channel effects pinned to their model, so the Model
  toggle cannot collapse the comparison. Shows *why* the models disagree:
  HSL saturation blows out at both ends (its `1 − |2L−1|` denominator), and
  HSL lightness sits uniformly below HSB brightness, widest where the image
  is most saturated.
- **`flat` neutrals** now threshold two ways at 50% instead of posterizing
  to three stops. The three-stop original survives as `Hue · flat · steps`.
- Also added from the hue-direction research: chroma, families, warm/cool,
  contours.

## Architecture: the effect library

- created [[decision-tile-effect-library]] — registry + shader dispatcher +
  `uTileTransform[9]`, so any effect sits on any tile. Right-click a tile
  for an in-place picker; RMB *drag* still pans (4px slop decides).
- created [[decision-chroma-neutral-detection]] — neutral detection moves
  from saturation to chroma across nine effects, with a tunable tolerance.

## Gotchas hit

- **Backticks inside a GLSL comment terminate the template literal.** Cost a
  confusing round of shader-compile errors before the parse error surfaced.
- **A hook added after an early return** crashed the canvas ("rendered more
  hooks than during the previous render"). The `if (!url) return` in
  `BreakdownCanvas` sits above the render body — new store selectors belong
  at the top with the others.
- **Raw `<select>` is white-on-white in this theme** — the native option
  list paints on the browser's own white popup while inheriting the page's
  light `text-foreground`. Taylor caught it. The project already had an
  unused `components/ui/select.tsx` (base-ui); that is the answer. Names
  then truncated to ambiguity at three columns, so effects carry a `short`
  label and the panel widened `w-80` → `w-96`.
- Synthetic `PointerEvent`s do not drive R3F's hover state, so the
  right-drag-does-not-open-menu path could only be verified by construction,
  not end to end.

## Noticed, not fixed
